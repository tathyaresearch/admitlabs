# Brand logos: sources and licence

Drishti shows the official one-colour logo of only three platforms, unchanged. Every other platform
(Google, Google Maps, Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Facebook, LinkedIn, Reddit, Quora) shows a
neutral line icon and its name, until AdmitLabs has each brand's permission.

Downloaded on 1 October 2026 from Simple Icons 16.33.0 (https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0). The files
are unchanged; `src/graphics/brands.ts` copies their path data as it is.

**LinkedIn, website footer only (9 October 2026):** the footer links to AdmitLabs' own LinkedIn,
X and Instagram pages with their marks, as the user asked. LinkedIn's "in" mark is drawn by hand
(`LINKEDIN_MARK` in `src/graphics/brands.ts`; Simple Icons no longer carries it); LinkedIn's
guidelines let a company use it to link to its own LinkedIn page. The footer draws all three in its
own link colour (slate, ivory under the pointer), to match the footer. Everywhere else LinkedIn
still gets a line icon.

The logos remain trademarks of their owners. Simple Icons' licence covers the files, not the right
to use the marks. Each is used as its owner's guidelines allow:

- **Instagram** (Meta brand resources): the glyph may be shown in any one solid colour, its shape
  unchanged. Drishti uses its text colour.
- **X** (X brand toolkit): the logo in black or white only. Drishti uses white on dark surfaces and
  black on light ones.
- **YouTube** (brand.youtube): the one-colour icon in white with a see-through play button on dark
  surfaces, or in almost black (#282828) with a white play button on light ones. Shown next to
  links to YouTube content, as YouTube's guidelines ask.

Nothing here accepts any brand's terms. Before using any other brand's logo, AdmitLabs gets that
brand's permission.
